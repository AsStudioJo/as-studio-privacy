DimiVue — Privacy Policy

Photo Measurement Studio · AS Studio

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Application: DimiVue: Photo Measurements
Android package: com.asstudiojo.dimivue
Developer: AS Studio
Privacy contact: AS.Studio.Jo@gmail.com

Summary: DimiVue is a local photo-annotation tool. You select images and manually add dimension lines, measurements and labels. The current app stores its photo projects on your device. AS Studio does not operate a server that receives your selected photos or project contents.

1. Information the app accesses

DimiVue accesses photos or files that you explicitly select using Android's photo or file picker. This access is used to display images, draw measurements, edit annotations and save your local projects. A selected photo may itself contain personal information. The app does not upload, remotely process or sell selected photos or project contents to AS Studio or other parties.

2. Project storage and exports

Selected photo copies, drawing coordinates, dimension values, units, styles, project titles and preferences may be stored in the app's local private storage so you can reopen and edit your work. Exports, where supported, are saved only to a location you choose. These files are not sent to AS Studio servers.

Local data is retained on your device until you remove it. App project controls can remove projects from your workspace; clearing DimiVue's app data or uninstalling the application removes its private local storage under Android's normal behavior. Any separately exported files remain where you saved them until you delete them.

3. Account, ads, purchases and analytics

The current DimiVue testing version does not require an account and does not include an advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or in-app purchases. It does not request or store credit-card information. If advertising, optional paid features or additional third-party services are introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's Google Play declarations will be updated before that release.

4. Collection, sharing and third parties

AS Studio does not collect or share the local photos, measurements or project files used in the DimiVue editor. Android system components provide the document picker and operating-system storage mechanisms and may be subject to their own platform privacy practices.

If you voluntarily email AS Studio for support, your email address and any information you send will be used to respond to your request. That information is not collected automatically by DimiVue.

5. Permissions

When you select a photo, Android grants access to that selected item for the requested editing workflow. DimiVue uses this access only for the user-requested local import and editing features. The core editor does not require background scanning of your media library or access to your location.

6. Security, retention and deletion

The app uses Android's application storage features to keep local projects on your device. Protect your device and any exports containing sensitive information. DimiVue's core annotation workflow does not transmit your project content to an AS Studio server. Local content is retained until removed as described in Section 2.

7. Children

DimiVue is a general-purpose photo annotation utility, not a service designed to collect information from children. AS Studio does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

8. Your privacy choices and contact

You decide which photos to import, which dimensions to enter and which projects to keep. AS Studio cannot remotely access or delete your locally stored project files. For questions about this policy, or a privacy request concerning information you have voluntarily sent to us, contact AS.Studio.Jo@gmail.com.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated when DimiVue's functions or privacy practices change. Updates will be published at this same address with an updated effective date.
