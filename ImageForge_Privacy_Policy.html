Privacy Policy for ImageForge

Last updated: October 30, 2026

ImageForge (package name: com.asstudiojo.imageforge) is developed by AS Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how ImageForge handles selected images, advertising-related information, and device data.

Images and Local Processing

ImageForge allows users to select, preview, convert, resize, compress, and export images. Image conversion is performed locally on the device. AS Studio does not receive or upload users' selected images to AS Studio servers. Converted images are stored on the user's device and the application is designed not to overwrite the original images.

Accounts

ImageForge does not require account creation or sign-in for its core image processing tools.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

ImageForge is intended to be a free app supported by Google AdMob advertising. When advertisements are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect, process, or share data, including IP addresses, advertising identifiers, device or app information, advertising interactions, and diagnostics, for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted. This processing is governed by applicable provider policies and user consent or preferences where required.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob information for publishers.

Permissions and Storage

ImageForge accesses only images selected by users through Android file or photo selection features. It saves converted images locally on the device. Advertising functionality may require network access and access to device-related identifiers according to Android and Google requirements.

Data Retention and User Choices

AS Studio does not store selected images on its servers. Users can delete exported images using their device's gallery or file manager. Google and its partners manage retention of advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies. Users can manage advertising preferences through available Google and device privacy controls.

Children's Privacy

ImageForge is not specifically designed for children and is intended to be marketed to adults. The app does not request that children submit personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

Security

Local image processing avoids uploading selected images to AS Studio servers. Nevertheless, no device or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in ImageForge, its advertising integrations, or relevant requirements. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
