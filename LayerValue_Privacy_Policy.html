LayerValue Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026
Developer: AS Studio
Package: com.asstudiojo.layervalue
Contact: AS.Studio.Jo@gmail.com

1. Overview

LayerValue is a 3D-print cost, pricing, materials, inventory, job, and business-management application developed by AS Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use LayerValue.

2. Information stored locally

LayerValue may store information you enter or generate inside the app, including printer profiles, material profiles, stock and inventory records, print calculations, saved jobs, quotes, business settings, preferences, and related app data.

3. Files you choose

LayerValue may allow you to select files such as G-code or 3MF files for analysis or import. The app processes files you explicitly select. AS Studio does not automatically upload those files to an AS Studio server.

4. Advertising

LayerValue uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

5. Purchases

LayerValue may offer an optional "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play Billing.

6. Third-party services

LayerValue may use Google services including:

  • Google AdMob;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP);
  • Google Play Billing;
  • Google Play services required by those features.

7. Permissions and device access

LayerValue requests only the device access needed for enabled features. File access is initiated when you choose a file or use an import/export function. The app does not intentionally access unrelated personal files.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored LayerValue data generally remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data, restore or overwrite it, or uninstall the app.

9. Your privacy choices

Where required, LayerValue provides privacy and consent choices through Google UMP. Depending on applicable regulations and your location, you may be able to consent, decline consent, manage options, or reopen privacy choices.

10. International processing

Third-party service providers such as Google may process information in countries other than your own. Their processing is governed by their applicable privacy terms and legal safeguards.

11. Children's privacy

LayerValue is a productivity and business utility for 3D-printing users and is not designed or directed specifically to children. AS Studio does not knowingly operate a system intended to collect personal information directly from children.

12. Security

AS Studio uses reasonable measures to keep LayerValue's local application data within the app's normal Android storage environment. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LayerValue features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
