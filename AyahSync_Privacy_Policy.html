Privacy Policy for AyahSync

Last updated: September 27, 2026

AyahSync – Quran Video Studio is developed by AS Studio.

AyahSync is designed to help users create Quran videos by aligning Quran recitation audio or video with Quran text and providing video editing and export tools.

Information We Collect

AyahSync does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Media Files

AyahSync allows users to select audio and video files from their device for processing. Selected media is used only to provide the app's editing, synchronization, preview, and export functionality.

Media processing is performed locally on the user's device. AS Studio does not upload or store the user's selected audio, video, or exported media on its servers.

Quran Text and Audio Processing

AyahSync uses Quran text and audio processing functionality to assist with synchronization of Quran recitation and video content. Processing used for the app's core synchronization features is performed on the device.

Files Created by the App

The app may create and save project information and exported media on the user's device. These files remain under the user's control and can be removed by the user.

Permissions

AyahSync may request access to media or files when necessary for selecting, processing, saving, or exporting content. These permissions are used only for the functionality requested by the user.

Advertising and Purchases

AyahSync does not contain advertising and does not require a paid subscription to access its core functionality.

Third-Party Services

AyahSync does not intentionally transmit users' personal media to AS Studio servers. Android system components or services used to operate the app may be subject to their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

AyahSync is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because the app's core media processing is performed locally on the user's device, user-selected media is not intentionally transmitted to AS Studio servers for processing.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
